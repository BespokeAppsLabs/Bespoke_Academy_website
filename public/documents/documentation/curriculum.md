# Curriculum

## Overview
Bespoke Academy runs a rolling monthly programme for Grades 8-11 with two streams, Engineering and Media. No prior experience is needed. Students join any month and move up a level when they pass its checkpoint, so faster students finish sooner. Each unit is one weekend session plus about an hour of homework.

## Key Information
- Two streams: Engineering (4 levels, units 1-40, about 10 months) and Media (6 levels, units 1-52, about 13 months)
- Weekly weekend sessions (2+ hours), mixed-level classes
- The first 8 units have no hardware; units 1-6 are shared by both streams
- A checkpoint closes each level: projects finished, the student explains their own code, and a short practical task
- Monthly show-and-tell; a demo day every term
- Ages 13-17

## Common Questions
**Q: Does my child need prior programming experience?**
A: No. Every student starts at Level 1.

**Q: How long does the programme take?**
A: There is no fixed end date. At a typical pace Engineering takes about 10 months and Media about 13. Faster students can move up sooner.

**Q: Can my child change stream or tier?**
A: Yes, up to unit 8. Both streams share units 1-6, so nothing is lost.

## Detailed Information

### Engineering Level 1: Foundations (units 1-8)
Python, Git and GitHub, working with AI tools responsibly, and circuits in a browser simulator. Projects: text adventure game, quiz app with an AI helper, traffic light in the simulator.

### Engineering Level 2: Electronics & Microcontrollers (units 9-18)
Wire and program sensors and outputs on a real board. Base uses an Arduino Uno; Mid adds Wi-Fi with an ESP32 and a Raspberry Pi; High also drives motors and servos. Projects: plant moisture alarm, then a reaction-time game (Base), Wi-Fi weather station (Mid and High) and a servo pan-tilt with joystick (High).

### Engineering Level 3: AI & Connected Systems (units 19-28)
Train an image model, call AI from Python, work with data, and connect AI to hardware. Projects: rock-paper-scissors recogniser, then an AI study helper (Base), power-cut monitor with phone alert (Mid) or an obstacle-avoiding robot (High). Each student pitches a final-project idea.

### Engineering Level 4: Final Project (units 29-40)
Proposal, build, test and a live demo at the termly demo day. Base builds a software and AI project, Mid a connected device, High a robot.

### Media Level 1: Foundations (units 1-8)
Shared coding units with Engineering, then how digital images work, composition and light.

### Media Level 2: Images (units 9-16)
Image recreation, image generation, editing with AI, and generating images from Python. Ethics and the law of AI images.

### Media Level 3: Characters & Scenes (units 17-24)
Character design and sheets, keeping a character consistent, scene creation, storyboarding, and a coded character bible.

### Media Level 4: Motion (units 25-32)
Image to video, camera moves, editing with sound and captions, motion graphics in code, and video exports with Python.

### Media Level 5: Blender 3D (units 33-40)
Modelling, materials, lighting and animation in Blender, turning AI images into 3D models, using renders to guide AI images and video, and Blender scripting in Python.

### Media Level 6: Final Project (units 41-52)
A 60-90-second piece bringing together a consistent character, Blender, motion graphics and a tool the student coded, screened at demo day.

# Projects

## Overview
Students build a project at every level and finish with a final project of their own, shown at a termly demo day. Every project goes on the student's GitHub portfolio. The explain-your-code rule applies to all of them: students must be able to explain any code they hand in, line by line.

## Key Information
- Engineering: 9 small projects across Levels 1-3, then a final project
- Media: 5 small projects (M1-M5) across Levels 1-5, then a final project
- Final projects are individual; two students may share a bigger project if each owns a clear part
- Mid and High final projects get up to R300 and R500 in parts, and use the academy 3D printer

## Common Questions
**Q: What kinds of projects will students create?**
A: Engineering students build games, sensor alarms, Wi-Fi devices, AI recognisers and robots. Media students make image series, comics, animated shorts, 3D scenes and a short film or music video.

**Q: Can students keep their projects?**
A: Yes. All code and media go on the student's own portfolio. Final-project parts bought with the academy allowance belong to the student once the project is done.

## Detailed Information

### Engineering small projects
1. Text adventure game (Level 1)
2. Quiz app with an AI helper (Level 1)
3. Traffic light in the simulator (Level 1)
4. Plant moisture alarm (Level 2)
5. Wi-Fi weather station (Level 2, Mid and High; Base builds a reaction-time game)
6. Servo pan-tilt with joystick (Level 2, High)
7. Rock-paper-scissors recogniser (Level 3)
8. Power-cut monitor with phone alert (Level 3, Mid; Base builds an AI study helper)
9. Obstacle-avoiding or line-following robot (Level 3, High)

### Engineering final project (Level 4)
- Base: software and AI, e.g. a smart study timer or a school-info chatbot
- Mid: a connected device, e.g. a home energy monitor or air-quality station
- High: a robot, e.g. a delivery rover or a sorting arm with a camera

### Media small projects
- M1 Photo study (Level 1)
- M2 Recreate and reimagine (Level 2)
- M3 6-panel story with one consistent character (Level 3)
- M4 30-second animated short (Level 4)
- M5 The character's world in 3D (Level 5)

### Media final project (Level 6)
A 60-90-second piece: a short film, an animated music video, an ad for a local business (with its permission) or a trailer for an imagined world.

### Final-project marking
- It works or delivers what the proposal promised: 30%
- Process and documentation: 25%
- Code quality and Git history: 15%
- Presentation and demo: 15%
- Originality and real-world impact: 15%
